# 提示词模板——阶段 6 · 每个资产的 360° 旋转视频

**模型：** Seedance 2.0（首选，orbit 模式）| Veo（备选）| Kling（用户要求时）
**时长：** 4-6 秒
**画幅：** 1:1 正方形（资产偏高时用 9:16——角色全身、武器）
**参考图：** 资产的静帧（始终作为首帧锚点附上）

## 硬性规则

**机位锁定，主体旋转。** 这与多数视频模型的默认行为相反——它们倾向于推拉镜头、加电影感运动、加光照漂移。这些都会毁掉参考用的 360。

用户要的输出等同于 Blender / Marmoset 中的转台渲染：主体居中，完整旋转一周，没有其他运动。

## 提示词模板

```
Locked-camera turntable rotation of the subject in the reference image. The camera does NOT move — it is fixed on a tripod at eye-level distance. The subject rotates a smooth full 360 degrees around its own vertical axis, starting facing the camera, rotating clockwise back to facing the camera at the end of the clip. White background remains pure white throughout. Lighting stays neutral and flat throughout — no rim light appearing, no shadow shifting dramatically. No zoom, no dolly, no pan, no tilt. No camera shake. No motion blur on the subject. Sharp focus across the entire rotation. Reference-grade turntable, identical to a 3D software preview render.
```

## 各资产覆盖写法

### 完整角色 360
```
Locked-camera turntable of the full character from the reference image. Subject stays in A-pose throughout the rotation. Camera fixed at eye-level, framing the full body from head to toe in the center of the frame. Smooth clockwise 360 over 5 seconds. White background, neutral flat lighting throughout. No pose changes, no expression changes — pure rotation only.
```

### 配饰 360（小物件——戒指、首饰、道具）
```
Locked-camera turntable of the {ITEM_NAME} from the reference image. Object placed centered in the frame, rotating smoothly around its vertical axis a full 360 over 4 seconds. White background. Soft neutral lighting from above, no harsh shadow. No motion of the camera. Sharp macro focus held across the rotation.
```

### 长条资产 360（武器、法杖、披风）
```
Locked-camera turntable of the {ITEM_NAME} from the reference image. Object held vertically in the center of the frame, rotating smoothly around its long axis a full 360 over 5 seconds. White background. Neutral flat lighting. No camera motion, no zoom.
```

## 通用负面提示词
```
no camera motion, no dolly, no zoom, no pan, no tilt, no shake, no cinematic lighting changes, no shadow drift, no rim light appearing mid-rotation, no environment, no background change, no pose change for the character, no expression change, no motion blur, no DoF breathing
```

## 为什么这是最难的一步

视频模型被训练成制作电影感视频——运动镜头、变化的光线、戏剧化镜头。参考转台恰恰相反：它是技术渲染，除主体旋转外一切都必须完全静止。

若 Seedance/Veo 尝试 2 次后仍不遵守“锁定机位”，可以：
1. 加强锁定机位的措辞：“tripod-mounted security camera locked to one frame”
2. 明确加入反运动描述：“the only motion in the video is the subject rotating”
3. 把时长缩到 3 秒（模型加入漂移的时间更少）
4. 若 Seedance 无法做到，退回使用“orbit mode”的 Veo

## 成本 / 预算

每个 360 = 1 次视频渲染。8 个部件 + 1 个完整角色的完整包 = 9 次渲染。在关卡 2 渲染之前向用户报总成本。提供“只做完整角色”作为最便宜的精简选项。

## 每个视频的质量检查清单

- [ ] 机位静止——画面边缘无漂移
- [ ] 背景保持纯白——无阴影移动、无环境
- [ ] 光照保持中性——无中途出现的轮廓光，主光不变色
- [ ] 主体完成完整 360（结束时再次正对镜头）
- [ ] 角色 360 中无姿势 / 表情变化
- [ ] 主体无运动模糊（旋转应平滑，但每帧都清晰）
- [ ] 若 2 次尝试失败：退回 Veo、缩短时长、简化提示词
